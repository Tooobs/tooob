# Licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0

tooob is licensed `MIT OR Apache-2.0`, as iroh and most of the Rust ecosystem are, and each user picks one: MIT for its simplicity and GPLv2 compatibility, Apache-2.0 for its patent grant. We rejected AGPL-3.0, which would make anyone running a modified Relay or Host as a service publish their changes. It would deter competing networks, but it would also deter the companies and contributors a young project needs, and what makes a network worth using is how well it's run. Hard to reverse: relicensing needs every contributor's consent.
